Task 1 — Stability Characterisation
Task: Your pipeline has now narrowed to five clinical candidates: D18, D19, D20, D21 and D22. Before formulation work begins, each needs its physicochemical stability characterised. Raw stress-stability data for all 100 original compounds is available for reference/download in each sub-task below, but focus your calculations on your five candidates. Complete all four sub-tasks.
Task 1a — Anhydrous (Solid-State) Stability
Stress degradation study, anhydrous conditions (closed vials), at 40°C / 60°C / 80°C over 0–8 weeks (HPLC purity, % area, mean of duplicate injections). For each candidate, determine the degradation rate constant at each temperature (k, wk-1), then use an Arrhenius plot (ln k vs 1/T) to calculate the activation energy (Ea) and extrapolate to predict k at 25°C and the shelf-life (t90, weeks to 90% purity remaining). PASS criterion: t90 ≥ 2 years.
Download full 100-compound reference dataset (.csv)
Task 1b — Aqueous Solution Stability (pH-Rate Profile)
Degradation rate constants (kobs, h-1) determined at 9 buffer pH values (25°C, ionic strength 0.5 M KCl). Plot the pH-rate profile (log kobs vs pH) for each candidate to identify the pH of maximum stability (minimum kobs), then calculate t90 at physiological pH (7.4) using t90 = 0.1054 / kobs(pH 7.4). PASS criterion: t90 ≥ 2 years.
Download full 100-compound reference dataset (.csv)
Task 1c — Acid (Gastric) Stability
Simulated Gastric Fluid (SGF, pH 1.2), 37°C. % remaining by HPLC at 0 / 15 / 30 / 60 / 120 min. Calculate the degradation rate constant (kacid) and half-life (t½) in SGF for each candidate. Classification: Stable >90%; Moderate 70–90%; Labile <70% remaining at 120 min.
Download full 100-compound reference dataset (.csv)
Task 1d — Photostability (ICH Q1B)
ICH Q1B photostability testing (D65 lamp), 25°C. % remaining vs dark control at 0 / 200 / 400 / 800 / 1200 Wh/m². Calculate the degradation rate constant (kphoto) for each candidate. ICH Q1B Classification: Photostable ≥95%; Photosensitive 80–95%; Photolabile <80% remaining at 1200 Wh/m².
Download full 100-compound reference dataset (.csv)
