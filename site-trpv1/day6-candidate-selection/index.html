Task 1 — Candidate Selection
Task: This is the final decision point. You have predicted human PK, preclinical single- and repeat-dose toxicity, the maximum recommended starting dose for first-in-human and predicted exposure and safety margins for all six candidates. Select up to three to progress, using your CDTP criteria, then justify your choice below.

Rat PK is from Day 2 (for the Task 2 compounds, the values you calculated from the raw data). Predicted human PK uses simple allometric scaling from a 0.25 kg rat to a 70 kg human: CL scaled by (0.25/70)0.25 per kg, Vd and %F taken as in the rat, t½ = 0.693 × Vd / CL. Once-daily dosing is judged feasible if the predicted t½ is 12 h or more (borderline at 8–12 h). The rat 7-day NOAEL and hERG IC50 are from Day 3; single-dose MTDs and the dog studies are new. Human equivalent doses (HED) are NOAEL ÷ 6.2 (rat) and NOAEL ÷ 1.8 (dog); the maximum recommended starting dose (MRSD) uses the most sensitive species: lower HED ÷ 10 (safety factor) × 70 kg, rounded down. Predicted Cmax is for oral dosing at the MRSD (one-compartment model, absorption rate constant 1 h−1), after a single dose and at steady state on once-daily dosing. The hERG margin is the hERG IC50 divided by the free (unbound) Cmax at steady state, converted to µM using the molecular weight.
