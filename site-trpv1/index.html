TRPV1 Antagonist Programme

Drug Discovery Simulation — a six-day undergraduate pharmacology workshop series

Before you start

Over six days you'll take a set of 100 fictional compounds through a drug discovery pipeline for TRPV1 (transient receptor potential vanilloid 1), an ion channel target for chronic pain — from initial channel pharmacology data through to selecting a clinical candidate. You are not given cut-off criteria in advance. Before each day, form your own Target Product Profile (TPP) and Candidate Drug Target Profile (CDTP) criteria, apply them to the data you're given, and be ready to justify your decisions. Your criteria can and should evolve as you see more data.

Enter a name or ID above before starting — this isn't a login, just a label so your facilitator can match your work to you when reviewing it later. Nothing is scored automatically on this site; each page lets you save your work and export it to submit through your course's usual submission system.

Day 1

Ion Channel Pharmacology

Fit a Ca²⁺ flux IC50 curve, compare orthogonal channel assays, then screen all 100 compounds on potency and subtype selectivity.
Day 2

ADME & Metabolic Stability

Filter on solubility, permeability and three distinct metabolic liability mechanisms across the four chemical series.
Day 3

In Vivo PK & Pain Models

Calculate PK parameters from raw concentration-time data, screen on rat PK and analgesic efficacy, then apply in vitro and in vivo safety criteria — including the class-defining hyperthermia liability.
Day 4

Chemical & Formulation Stability

Calculate stability kinetics from raw data, then decide on formulation strategy for the clinical candidates.
Day 5

Metabolite Identification

Assign NMR and MS/MS spectra for the clinical candidates and their metabolites.
Day 6

Clinical Candidate Selection

Select up to three candidates to progress, with a written justification referencing your TPP/CDTP criteria.
