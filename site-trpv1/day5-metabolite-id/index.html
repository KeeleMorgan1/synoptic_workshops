Task 1 — Spectral Assignment
Task: Each of your six candidates has one Phase 1 (oxidative or hydrolytic) metabolite; two of them (D16, D19) go on to a Phase 2 (glucuronide conjugate) metabolite; a third (D22) also forms a Phase 2 conjugate. Open the spectra tool below and assign every ¹H NMR peak and MS/MS fragment — for the parent compounds first, then use them as your reference to work out what structural change occurred in each metabolite. Your answers in the tool save automatically, exactly like the rest of this site.

Metabolites shown below have had their reaction type, enzyme and site of metabolism withheld — working those out from the spectra is the point of the exercise. Three of the Phase 1 metabolites (D19-M1, D20-M1, D22-M1) share the same [M+H]⁺ mass and molecular formula — you'll need both MS/MS and NMR to tell them apart. Their two respective Phase 2 conjugates (D19-M2 and D22-M2) are also isobaric with each other.
