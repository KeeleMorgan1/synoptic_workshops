BACE1 Inhibitor Programme

Drug Discovery Simulation — a six-day undergraduate pharmacology workshop series

Before you start

Over six days you'll take a 100-compound BACE1 inhibitor library (five chemical series, A–E) through a drug discovery pipeline for Alzheimer's disease — from in vitro potency and selectivity through to selecting a clinical candidate. Days 1–3 triage the full library down to a shortlist (100 → potency/selectivity screen → ADME/PK screen → in vivo pharmacology screen); Days 4–6 profile the shortlisted candidates — including D18–D22, which you'll examine in depth throughout — in stability, metabolism and clinical candidate selection. You are not given cut-off criteria in advance. Before each day, form your own Target Product Profile (TPP) and Candidate Drug Target Profile (CDTP) criteria, apply them to the data you're given, and be ready to justify your decisions. Your criteria can and should evolve as you see more data.

Enter a name or ID above before starting — this isn't a login, just a label so your facilitator can match your work to you when reviewing it later. Nothing is scored automatically on this site; each page lets you save your work and export it to submit through your course's usual submission system.

Day 1

In Vitro Potency & Selectivity

Calculate pIC50 from concentration-response data, fit Hill curves, then assess selectivity against BACE2, Cathepsin D/B and hERG — then triage a full 100-compound screen.
Day 2

ADME & In Vivo PK

Classify permeability and metabolic stability, calculate PK parameters from raw iv/po rat data, assess CNS penetration, then triage a 63-compound ADME/PK screen.
Day 3

In Vivo Pharmacology & Toxicology

Screen 22 compounds for CSF/brain Aβ40 lowering to shortlist 5, then cognitive rescue and cardiac, hepatic, genotoxic and retinal safety assessment.
Day 4

Stability & Formulation

Calculate stability kinetics from raw solid-state, aqueous, acid and photostability data, then plan formulation strategy.
Day 5

Metabolite Identification

Assign NMR and MS/MS spectra for eight metabolites across the five candidates, including an isobaric pair and direct Phase 2 glucuronidation.
Day 6

Clinical Candidate Selection

Select a clinical candidate from those carried forward, with a written justification referencing your TPP/CDTP criteria, and a first-in-human dose prediction.
