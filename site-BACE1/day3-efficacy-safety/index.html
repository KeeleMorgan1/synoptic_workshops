Task: Using D22's discrimination index (DI) from the table above, calculate the percentage improvement in DI vs vehicle, and comment on its translational significance. Discuss why in vivo pharmacology studies should be run under matched dosing conditions to the PK studies.

DI = (time with novel object − time with familiar object) / (time novel + time familiar). Vehicle DI = 0.50 in all groups; % improvement vs vehicle = (DI₂₀ − 0.50) / 0.50 × 100.

Task 4 — Hepatic, Genotoxic & Retinal Safety
Task: Assess hepatic safety for each compound, rank compounds by HepG2 cytotoxicity, and estimate retinal risk from BACE2 selectivity and free brain exposure. Negative Ames/micronucleus results are given — discuss what this does and doesn't tell you.

BACE2 substrates include PMEL (melanocyte differentiation) — high BACE2 inhibition at clinical exposures raises risk of retinal depigmentation. BACE2:BACE1 selectivity ≥50× is a key safety criterion. Rat 7-day NOAEL ≥50 mg/kg and ALT fold-change <2× are standard tolerability bars.
