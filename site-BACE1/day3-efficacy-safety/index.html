Task 2 — In Vitro Safety Screen
Task: The shortlisted lead compounds have been profiled in a panel of in vitro safety assays: hERG electrophysiology (patch-clamp IC50), the AMES test, an in vitro micronucleus test, HepG2 hepatocellular viability, and BACE2:BACE1 selectivity. Off-target BACE2 inhibition at clinical exposure is linked to retinal depigmentation (via PMEL processing in the eye), so BACE2:BACE1 selectivity is a key safety parameter for this target class.
