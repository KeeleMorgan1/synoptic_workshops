Task 2 — Rat In Vivo Pharmacokinetics
Task: You have been given iv and po pharmacokinetic data for 5 compounds from the Lead Optimisation series. Plot Time vs [drug] graphs on both linear and log[drug] axes, and use the data to calculate: C0 (concentration at t=0), Vd (volume of distribution), Ke (elimination rate constant), T½ (plasma half-life), Cl (systemic clearance), F% (oral bioavailability), Cmax (oral maximum plasma concentration) and Tmax (time Cmax is achieved). Paste your graphs and submit your calculations below.

Assay: plasma concentrations (ng/mL) by LC-MS/MS, iv dose 3 mg/kg (bolus, n=3), po dose 30 mg/kg (gavage, n=3). Values are group means.

Task 3 — Pharmacokinetic Screen
Task: You now have rat in vivo pharmacokinetic data for all the compounds from the ADME screen in Task 1. Use your CDTP criteria to screen the compounds below, then answer the prompts underneath. Judge each compound on the PK criteria alone, whatever your Task 1 decision.

In practice you would not run in vivo PK on compounds that failed in vitro screening (cost, and the 3Rs); the full dataset is given here so you can test how well your in vitro criteria predicted in vivo performance. Includes rat iv clearance, volume of distribution, half-life, oral bioavailability, plasma free fraction and brain penetration (Kp and Kp,uu).
