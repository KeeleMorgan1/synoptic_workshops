Task 1 — ADME Screen
Task: You have received a lecture on, and been given CDTP criteria for, the in vitro tests used to investigate ADME characteristics of novel pharmaceutical compounds. You now have access to data for the compounds that survived Day 1. Apply the criteria you've been given to filter out compounds not meeting them, colour-coding each compound in the table below, then answer the prompts underneath.

This programme is unusually Phase II-dominant: alongside kinetic solubility, LogD7.4, Caco-2 permeability and human liver microsomal (HLM, Phase I) stability, you're also given UGT glucuronidation half-life/clearance, SULT sulphation extent, whether a glucuronide was directly detected, a chlorine isotope pattern flag (mass spectrometry diagnostic for the C-series), and GSH reactive metabolite trapping. A compound can look perfectly stable on HLM t½ and still be rapidly cleared via Phase II routes — don't rely on HLM alone.
