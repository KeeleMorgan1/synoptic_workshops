How to use this exercise

For each candidate, the parent compound is given (structure, formula, and reference ¹H NMR / MS/MS spectra). Each candidate then has one proposed Phase I metabolite, and three candidates (D15, D21, D22) additionally have a Phase II conjugate derived from that Phase I metabolite. These metabolites are presented as unknowns: only their simulated spectra and peak tables are shown. Using the parent's spectra as a reference, propose the metabolite's structure, the likely enzyme/pathway, and the site of biotransformation.

Tip: four of the five parent structures (D19-D22) share a common difluoroanilino-aminopyrimidine-carboxamide “core” and a C6 aryl/heteroaryl “tail” cap; D15 alone has no C6 cap. In the MS/MS data, compare which fragment (Core+H⁺ or Tail+H⁺) has shifted mass relative to the parent — that tells you which half of the molecule was modified. Watch the direction of the mass shift too: two metabolites in this set (D20-M1 and D22-M1) show a mass decrease despite being genuine CYP3A4 oxidations. And a coincidence worth knowing about: D19-M1 and D20-M1 (from two different parents) turn out to be exactly isobaric — a reminder that mass alone never proves which parent a metabolite came from. Glucuronide (Phase II) spectra show a diagnostic [M+H−176]⁺ neutral-loss ion that reproduces the corresponding Phase I metabolite's own [M+H]⁺ mass exactly.

D15 PDE4 IC50 17.0 nM; BD ratio 2.4x (lower than the other candidates) -- not recommended, borderline PDE4D-sparing selectivity is the main concern.

Technical notes & provenance

  • All 5 parent structures are taken directly from the existing compound library (Workshop 6 clinical candidates D15/D19/D20/D21/D22). All metabolite structures, exact masses, MS/MS fragment assignments and ¹H NMR peak lists were newly derived for this workshop (RDKit-validated formulas and exact masses; hand-assigned NMR shifts using standard empirical correlation ranges).
  • Every metabolite's total ¹H count reconciles exactly with its calculated molecular formula (verified programmatically).
  • MS/MS fragmentation model: each parent/metabolite is split into a shared difluoroanilino-aminopyrimidine-carboxamide “Core” fragment and a C6 aryl/heteroaryl (or, for D15 only, exocyclic amide) “Tail” fragment; whichever side carries the proposed biotransformation shows the corresponding mass shift, letting students localise the change from the fragment pattern alone. Glucuronide conjugates show the classic [M+H−176]⁺ neutral loss of anhydroglucuronic acid.
  • Relative intensities, exact m/z values, and NMR chemical shifts are illustrative teaching constructs, not measured or literature-derived data. All compounds are entirely fictional.
  • Spectra are rendered as inline SVG from embedded data — no external libraries or internet connection required.
