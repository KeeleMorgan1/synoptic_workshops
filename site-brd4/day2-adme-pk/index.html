Task 1 — ADME & In Vivo PK Screen
Task: You have received a lecture on, and been given CDTP criteria for, the in vitro ADME and in vivo pharmacokinetic tests used to characterise novel pharmaceutical compounds. You now have access to data for the compounds that survived Day 1. Apply the criteria you've been given to filter out compounds not meeting them, colour-coding each compound in the table below, then answer the prompts underneath.

Includes Caco-2 permeability, efflux ratio, P-gp substrate status and CYP3A4 inhibition (in vitro ADME), alongside rat iv clearance, volume of distribution, iv half-life, oral bioavailability and plasma protein binding (in vivo PK). BD1 Kd and MV4-11 IC50 are carried over from Day 1 for reference.

Task 2 — Rat In Vivo Pharmacokinetics
Task: You have been given iv and po pharmacokinetic data for 10 compounds from the Lead Optimisation series (the five clinical candidates plus five series comparators). Plot Time vs [drug] graphs on both linear and log[drug] axes, and use the data to calculate: C0 (concentration at t=0), Vd (volume of distribution), Ke (elimination rate constant), T½ (plasma half-life), Cl (systemic clearance), F% (oral bioavailability), Cmax (oral maximum plasma concentration) and Tmax (time Cmax is achieved). Paste your graphs and submit your calculations below.

Assay: plasma concentrations (ng/mL) by LC-MS/MS, iv dose 1 mg/kg (bolus, n=3), po dose 10 mg/kg (gavage, n=3). Values are group means. Only the raw concentration-time data is given here — no pre-calculated PK parameters.
