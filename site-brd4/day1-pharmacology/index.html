Task 2 — Cellular Functional Assay
Task: You have been given data from a cellular c-MYC suppression assay for 4 compounds, including the positive control (+)-JQ1. Plot graphs for the data (all four compounds can go on one graph if you like). Write a one-page report outlining the assay used, and interpreting your results. Your interpretation should discuss the potency and efficacy of the three unknown compounds.

Assay: Cellular c-MYC suppression assay (MV4-11 cells, in-cell Western, normalised to total protein), 8-point concentration-response. MYC suppression is the functionally relevant, disease-linked readout — it integrates cell permeability and target engagement inside the cell, and is typically right-shifted relative to the biochemical BD1 Kd.
