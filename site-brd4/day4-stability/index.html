Task 1 — Stability Characterisation
Task: Your pipeline has now narrowed to five clinical candidates: D18, D19, D20, D21 and D22. Before formulation work begins, each needs its physicochemical stability characterised. Raw stress-stability data for all 100 original compounds is available for reference/download in each sub-task below, but focus your calculations on your five candidates. Complete all four sub-tasks.
Task 1a — Anhydrous (Solid-State) Stability
Stress degradation study, anhydrous dry powder (protected from light), at 40°C / 60°C / 80°C over 0–12 weeks (HPLC-UV purity, % area). For each candidate, determine the degradation rate constant at each temperature (k, wk-1), then use an Arrhenius plot (ln k vs 1/T) to calculate the activation energy (Ea) and extrapolate to predict k at 25°C and the shelf-life (t90, weeks to 90% purity remaining).
Download full 100-compound reference dataset (.csv)
Task 1b — Aqueous (Solution) Stability
% remaining (HPLC-UV) at pH 7.4 phosphate buffer, protected from light, across four temperatures (25°C / 40°C / 50°C / 60°C) over 0–14 days. Determine the degradation rate constant at each temperature, then use an Arrhenius plot to extrapolate k at 25°C and predict t90 at pH 7.4.
Download full 100-compound reference dataset (.csv)
Task 1c — Acid (Gastric) Stability
Forced degradation in 0.1N HCl (pH ∼1.1), 37°C, simulating gastric conditions. % remaining by HPLC-UV at 0 / 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 / 24 h. Calculate the degradation rate constant (kacid) and half-life for each candidate, and classify each as gastric-stable or acid-labile.
Download full 100-compound reference dataset (.csv)
Task 1d — Photostability (ICH Q1B)
ICH Q1B-style forced photodegradation with a parallel dark control. % remaining vs light exposure (0 / 0.5x / 1x / 1.5x / 2x ICH units) and the matched dark-control series. Calculate the degradation rate constant (kphoto) for each candidate and classify against the ICH Q1B thresholds.
